# ChemLab IGCSE Chemistry

An interactive app for **Cambridge IGCSE Chemistry (0620, 2026–2028 syllabus)**. It has animated lessons, the Bond Lab, a Periodic Table, an ions explorer, quizzes and exam-style practice papers.

## Get the app

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| **Windows (recommended)** | [⬇ Download ChemLab-IGCSE-Setup.exe](https://github.com/njabuloshong1/chemlab/releases/latest/download/ChemLab-IGCSE-Setup.exe). Installs with desktop and Start-menu shortcuts. No admin rights needed. |
| **Windows (no install)** | [⬇ Download ChemLab-IGCSE-Portable.exe](https://github.com/njabuloshong1/chemlab/releases/latest/download/ChemLab-IGCSE-Portable.exe). Double-click to run, e.g. from a USB stick. |

| **Chromebook / Android / Mac** | Open **https://njabuloshong1.github.io/chemlab/** in Chrome, then click the **install icon** in the address bar |

The app works **offline** after the first visit. Quiz scores are saved on each device.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. This appears because the app is not code-signed.

All downloads are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/njabuloshong1/chemlab/releases).

## What's inside

- **Journey into the Atom**: zoom from a copper wire into one atom and take it apart.
- **Bond Lab**: click any two elements to see how they bond, with the formula, name, balanced equation and an animation.
- **Periodic Table**: all 118 elements, with atoms, shells, ions and trends.
- **Ions & Formulae**: sulfate, nitrate, ammonium and more, plus a formula builder.
- **15 topics** covering all 12 syllabus sections, with 24 animations and 95 worked examples.
- **154 quiz questions** with explanations, and **3 exam-style practice papers** with mark schemes.

The practice papers are original questions written in the Cambridge style. Real past papers are copyright Cambridge University Press & Assessment.
